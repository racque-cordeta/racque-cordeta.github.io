About

My “friends” call me Rax. I’m a self-taught developer and cybersecurity enthusiast who thrives on turning “I don’t know how” into “I just built it.” My path hasn’t been a straight line. After leaving college, I faced a choice: let failure define me or let it refine me. I chose the latter. With nothing but pure curiosity and a mindset that refuses to quit, I taught myself to code, design, and deploy.

I specialize in the intersection of IT Infrastructure and Creative Design. Web: HTML, CSS, JAVASCRIPT, Flutter (for mobile/web), and UI Cloning. Security: Google-certified in Cybersecurity & IT Support. Tools: Python for automation, SQL for data, and Figma for design.

And right now, I’m not just looking for a job; I’m looking to solve problems. Whether it’s securing a network or building a seamless user experience, I bring a “day one” energy to every project. I’m currently focused on Full-Stack Development and Cybersecurity, constantly shipping new code and learning new frameworks.
